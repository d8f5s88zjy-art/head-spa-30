# V9 Media Production Brief — Higgsfield / Runway / OpenArt

V9 funguje aj bez nového AI média. Nasledujúce assety sú **voliteľné V9.1** a majú sa použiť až po vizuálnej kontrole. Kritický text, ceny a CTA zostávajú vždy v HTML.

## A. Hero poster — desktop

**Output:** 1920×1080, later AVIF/WebP, no text, no logo.

Prompt direction:
> Ultra-premium Japanese-inspired head spa interior in a real Central European salon, dark forest-green environment, black ceramic head-spa basin, elegant arc of warm water, soft steam, restrained 24K gold rim light, cinematic low-key lighting, tactile stone and brushed brass, calm luxury, authentic editorial beauty campaign, natural imperfections, no visible branding, no text, no surreal objects, no extra fingers, no exaggerated spa fantasy, wide composition with safe dark negative space on the left for HTML headline, subject/action in the right third.

## B. Hero poster — mobile

**Output:** 1080×1920. Dedicated composition, not a desktop crop.

Prompt direction:
> Same visual DNA as desktop. Vertical cinematic close composition: black basin lower centre, water arc entering from upper right, steam rising through warm gold light, dark green salon environment, enough clean negative space in upper-left/middle for HTML copy, no text or logo.

## C. Ritual close-ups

Generate 4:5 stills for: warm water, scalp massage hands, steamed towel, gold-light finale. Avoid identifiable staff unless real consent/photos are supplied. No fake testimonials or before/after claims.

## D. Optional 5–6 second transition video

Use Higgsfield/Runway only if the final web still passes performance without it.

Shot:
> camera moves slowly through dark green entrance threshold toward a black head-spa basin; water begins to flow; steam catches one thin gold light; camera settles rather than looping aggressively. No text, logo, face, or audio. First and final frames must both work as stills.

Delivery:
- desktop master 1920×1080
- mobile master 1080×1920
- silent WebM + H.264 MP4 derivatives
- poster still for each ratio
- first useful paint must not depend on video
- pause offscreen; reduced-motion uses poster only

## E. Quality rejection rules

Reject any generation with: glossy plastic skin, impossible hands, duplicated water fixtures, random Japanese characters, over-golden luxury clichés, spa stock-photo smile, purple/blue tech glow, fake signage, fake awards, text baked into the image, or visibly synthetic architecture.
