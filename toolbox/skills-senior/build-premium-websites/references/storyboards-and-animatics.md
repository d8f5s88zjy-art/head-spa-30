# 034 — Storyboard a animatik webu

Use when a cinematic sequence is too complex to judge from a moodboard. Treat the web as an interactive document with controllable pacing.

## Decide

Give each scene one visitor question and one visual answer. Choose the entry, readable hold and exit. Decide whether time, scroll or explicit interaction owns progress. Do not combine independent clocks without a synchronization contract.

## Execute

Draw a small sequence of stable frames with real copy and subject proportions. Annotate camera, focal point, foreground/background layers, masks, incoming/outgoing elements and action availability. Add mobile and reduced-motion frames before refining desktop spectacle. Mark asset dependencies and which frames can be replaced by a poster.

Build an animatic only to validate continuity and pacing. Specify how fast scrolling, reverse travel, direct entry and skip affect the story. A forward-only video is insufficient evidence for scroll behavior. Keep sound optional and ensure the intended idea remains understandable silently.

## Verify

Inspect start, overlap, hold and exit frames. Check that subject identity, reading order and action remain clear when the user pauses between planned beats. Test the proposed transition direction against the spatial relationship it implies. Flag impossible crops or missing imagery before implementation.

## Deliver

Provide frame names, progress/time boundaries, copy, shot notes, layer ownership, media needs and fallback. Use the detailed designer storyboard prompt to commission this package. Distinguish a visual proposal from an executed animation; hand off the logic that connects frames, not only attractive endpoints.
