# 098 — Produkčný rozsah a dodávatelia

Use when photographs, video, 3D, copy, licenses or specialist implementation must be sourced. Make deliverables inspectable before committing resources.

## Decide

Define whether the need is a finished asset, editable source, ongoing service or technical integration. Compare supplied material, commissioned production and generated illustration against truth, quality, cost, rights and future editing. Do not purchase or sign anything without authorization.

## Execute

Write a brief with purpose, real inputs, dimensions/ratios, technical constraints, required variants, rights, deadline, review rounds and acceptance. Include mobile crops, text-safe areas, reduced-motion/poster needs and source-file requirements where relevant. Use detailed designer prompts as the creative contract, adapted to the actual supplier.

Separate unknown content and policy decisions from creative freedom. Require references for claims, retain asset provenance and avoid requesting imitation of a whole competitor identity. Prepare reviewable options and estimates before seeking a genuine external commitment.

## Verify

Inspect delivered assets in the intended composition, not only a supplier preview. Check editable structure, exports, color/format correctness, license scope and compatibility with the runtime budget. Test a representative derivative before accepting a large batch.

## Deliver

Provide the production brief, selected option rationale, source/derivative inventory and acceptance findings. Record unresolved dependencies and who owns revisions. Do not treat a payment or a long prompt as evidence that the result meets the website's actual needs.
