# 086 — Testovanie prístupnosti

Use when accepting a user-facing flow or changing semantics, focus, contrast or motion. Match evidence to the claimed scope.

## Decide

Choose relevant pages, states, content variations and applicable criteria. Distinguish automated findings, manual interaction checks and specialist conformance review. A broad compliance claim needs broader evidence than this skill's routine checks.

## Execute

Combine automated inspection with keyboard navigation, focus visibility, labels, errors, content order, zoom/reflow, contrast and motion preferences. Test real flows rather than isolated attractive components. Include overlays, loading, rejected submissions and success messages.

Where possible, use a named screen-reader/browser combination for meaningful tasks. Keep canvas-heavy explanations available through semantic alternatives. Record build, environment, method, observation and correction. Treat tools as evidence collectors, not authorities that can certify the entire product by a score.

## Verify

Recheck the specific issue after correction and ensure the fix did not damage another input mode. A removed focus outline may make a screenshot cleaner while making navigation worse. Mark blocked assistive-technology/device checks honestly and continue independent work.

## Deliver

Provide tested scope, findings by user impact, fixes, unresolved issues and evidence limits. Reference current standards for precise requirements. Do not average away a blocked primary task because the rest of the page looks excellent or because an automated scanner reported no errors.
