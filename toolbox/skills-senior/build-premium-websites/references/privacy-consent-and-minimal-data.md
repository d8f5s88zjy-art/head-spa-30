# 078 — Súkromie, súhlas a minimum dát

Use when forms, analytics, embeds or accounts process personal information. Start from the actual data flow and jurisdiction.

## Decide

List what is collected, why, where it goes, who can access it and how long it remains. Distinguish necessary product operation from optional analytics/marketing. Determine which legal or policy decisions require current authoritative guidance rather than assumptions.

## Execute

Collect only data needed for the stated purpose. Keep sensitive values out of URLs, analytics events, screenshots and routine logs. Implement the project's actual consent/preferences policy consistently across scripts and embeds. Make changes or withdrawal meaningful rather than merely toggling a label.

Map deletion, export and retention behavior through providers where those obligations are in scope. Keep public privacy statements aligned with actual implementation and verified business policy. Do not invent legal wording or claim that a cookie banner alone establishes compliance.

## Verify

Inspect network behavior before and after the relevant preference, including reload and return visits. Check forms and events for unnecessary identifiers. Verify access to stored records and the documented retention/deletion path in an authorized test environment where applicable.

## Deliver

Provide the data-flow map, implemented controls, policy dependencies and observed tests. Browse current official sources for specific legal guidance and route consequential legal interpretation appropriately. Keep this chapter as engineering procedure, not a jurisdiction-independent legal certificate.
