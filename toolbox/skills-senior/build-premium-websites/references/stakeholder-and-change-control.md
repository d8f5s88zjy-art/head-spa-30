# 008 — Komunikácia a zmeny rozsahu

Use when several people influence the result or new requests change agreed work. Keep this proportional to project size and existing authorization.

## Decide

Identify who owns business truth, design direction, technical acceptance and release. Distinguish a correction to agreed behavior from additional scope. An inaccessible booking button is a defect; adding subscriptions may be a new product requirement. Do not use change control as an excuse to avoid routine fixes.

## Execute

Make review concrete: show the actual composition, functional slice or diff with the question it resolves. Summarize what changed, impact on the visitor, relevant evidence and one recommended next action. When feedback conflicts, identify the common objective and compare consequences. Resolve reversible details yourself; request a decision only where goals or authority genuinely conflict.

For a scope change record added outcome, affected dependencies, likely effort, risk and displaced work. Carry forward approvals already given in the session. Do not silently replace a delivered feature with a new request or send messages to stakeholders without explicit authorization.

## Verify

Before handing off, compare the implemented behavior with accepted requirements and the latest steering. Ensure unresolved decisions have an owner and impact, not just a vague “TBD”. Verify that users can inspect the thing they are being asked to approve; do preparatory work before a real release confirmation is needed.

## Deliver

Produce a short decision/update record suitable for the recipient. Use plain language and real observations, not “premium” as an explanation. For a solo small website, a few lines can carry the entire record. For a programme, preserve traceability across work packages without turning every spacing change into a committee decision.
