## 1. Generate meaningful alternatives

For a substantial redesign, make three compact candidates using actual content. They must differ in composition or narrative, not merely color. Use sketches, design files, locally authored studies or the appropriate image/design tools. If visuals cannot be rendered, label textual candidates as descriptions. Choose autonomously unless the user's decision is materially necessary.

Examples for a local service, to adapt rather than reuse blindly:

- **Documentary craft:** authentic detail as the dominant image; nearby concise offer; process and people as proof; sharp editorial divisions. Motion follows hands/materials only when real footage supports it.
- **Quiet editorial:** generous margins, a calm full-height crop, mixed portrait/landscape pacing, compact service rows. A small reveal or shared framing motif provides continuity.
- **Graphic identity:** a distinctive typographic grid, real photo windows, one recurring shape from the actual identity. Motion transforms that shape across chapters; do not invent a logo or visual meaning and call it established brand strategy.

For each candidate state: first focal point, next focal point, text/image relationship, actual asset needed, mobile reinterpretation, motion motif and one reason it may fail. Compare on the visitor job, brand fit and production constraints. A competitor's screenshot is not permission to copy their identity.

## 2. Compose stable frames

Capture or specify hero, proof, choice and final action at rest. Define a focal-point chain using subject scale, contrast and position. Keep a clear reading width within an expansive image composition. Use a repeated alignment line to connect sections without making them identical.

Use real text and test longest expected titles. Start body copy near a comfortable reading size and line length, then inspect at the target viewport; proposed values are design starting points, not standards. Do not shrink copy to protect an oversized heading. Tune letter spacing by role and typeface. Verify Slovak accents in all selected weights. Build a strong layout with fallback fonts before relying on a font download.

Write composition tokens as roles: `surface`, `ink`, `muted-ink`, `accent`, `on-accent`, `focus`, `content-max`, `reading-max`, `section-gap`, `display-size`, `body-leading`. Check every token's usage and contrast; a palette of hex values alone is not a system.

At mobile widths decide separately which crop survives, where the headline wraps, whether the media precedes the text, and whether a sticky action is necessary. Reserve space for the action and browser safe area. Test banners and virtual keyboards where relevant. Do not make a permanent bottom CTA hide content or focus.

## 3. Review with anchored criteria

Use a 0–3 internal rubric for critique, not a public quality certificate. Record an observed reason and a screenshot/state for each mark. `unobserved` is not 0 and cannot be averaged into a score.

| Dimension | 0: fails | 1: weak | 2: coherent | 3: distinctive and resolved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Offer/action not findable | Several competing focal points | Clear first/next action | Clear hierarchy with intentional contrast across sections |
| Typography | Unreadable/clipped | Generic or inconsistent roles | Legible, consistent roles | Optical spacing, line breaks and multilingual lengths resolved |
| Composition | Collisions/crop loses subject | Unrelated blocks | Grid, crop and rhythm coherent | Repeated motif links different layouts without monotony |
| Imagery | False/unsuitable evidence | Weak source or inconsistent grade | Authentic, useful and consistent | Subject, light and negative space actively support the story |
| Interaction | Main path fails | Unclear affordances or unstable states | Predictable complete states | Precise feedback and useful continuity under interruption |
| Mobile | Essential flow broken | Desktop compressed | Purposeful reflow and touch use | Recomposition preserves identity and information priority |

Do not compensate a broken path, false claim or inaccessible control with a high visual mark. A reviewer must distinguish aesthetic judgment from measurable behavior. Two reviewers may disagree; preserve the concrete observations and resolve the highest-impact disagreement in the artifact.

## 4. Correction loop

Inspect a page overview for rhythm, then actual reading scale for typography and controls. Choose the three most important defects. For each write `observed defect → why it harms the visitor → one change → same-condition comparison`. Keep viewport, content and state constant in before/after comparisons. Do not add visual noise when the failure is missing evidence, weak copy or unsuitable imagery.

Use two targeted correction passes as an initial allocation, not an endless requirement. If the result is still weak, reassess the underlying direction or source media. Record the limitation instead of accumulating effects. Never fabricate screenshots or user-test observations.

## Senior decision gate

- **Decision:** Compare genuinely different compositions against the visitor job.
- **Deliverable:** A selected direction with observed corrections.
- **Verification:** Do not invent comparison scores when no baseline was inspected.
