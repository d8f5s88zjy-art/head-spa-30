# 049 — Práva a autenticita podkladov

Use when selecting photographs, video, models, fonts, illustrations or generated imagery. Preserve truth and traceable origin without adding unnecessary bureaucracy.

## Decide

Classify assets as real business evidence, licensed illustration, generated concept or placeholder. Use authentic media for actual premises, people, products and services unless the user explicitly requests a clearly understood transformation. Do not silently turn a generated scene into a claimed customer result.

## Execute

Record source, owner, permitted use, editing restrictions, attribution and editable master. Check the actual license for the intended commercial distribution; a catalog listing is not proof of rights. Keep personal/client media within the authorized workflow. Avoid sending it to additional services unless that use is authorized.

Make derivatives with recorded crops, retouching and generated additions. Preserve product markings and characteristics that matter to the buyer. A mockup should be labeled as a mockup where mistaken reality would mislead. When legal interpretation is necessary, verify current jurisdiction-specific sources and route concrete uncertainties appropriately.

## Verify

Compare final assets with originals for unwanted alterations, misleading before/after claims and lost credits. Check whether fonts, stock or model licenses cover the planned delivery. Inspect alt text and captions for invented identities or unsupported context.

## Deliver

Provide an asset manifest, source/derivative relationship, credits and unresolved rights dependencies. Keep originals replaceable. Never assume generated imagery guarantees exclusivity or legal clearance; report what the actual tool/license and authorized inputs establish.
