# 093 — Údržba a zastarávanie

Use when the site must remain usable after delivery. Maintenance is an ownership and change process, not only dependency updates.

## Decide

Identify owners for content, integrations, dependencies, infrastructure and credentials. Set a realistic support scope and review cadence based on consequences. Separate urgent vulnerabilities or broken provider contracts from optional cosmetic updates.

## Execute

Record version assumptions, renewal/expiry dependencies, backup/recovery and the critical journey checks. Watch official deprecations relevant to installed tools when the maintenance task is active. Upgrade in bounded steps with a rollback route and targeted verification.

Keep content truth current: hours, services, prices and booking destinations can become stale independently of code. Remove obsolete experiments and unused assets deliberately. Preserve original sources and enough design/motion context for another maintainer to make changes without reverse engineering the entire site.

## Verify

Exercise the affected journey after each material change. Recheck provider connectivity, permission boundaries and rendering compatibility when those dependencies move. Do not imply ongoing background monitoring or scheduled work unless an actual authorized automation/service exists.

## Deliver

Provide ownership, maintenance tasks, support boundaries, known deprecations and update evidence. Keep the handoff usable by the actual team. A premium website's value includes the ability to change a price or replace a photograph without rebuilding its core experience.
