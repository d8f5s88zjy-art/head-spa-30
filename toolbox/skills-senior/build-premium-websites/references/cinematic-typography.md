# 039 — Filmová typografia a kinetický text

Use when text itself carries a narrative beat. Preserve comprehension and semantic content while adding movement.

## Decide

Choose a small phrase worth emphasis and a motion metaphor connected to its meaning. Distinguish an editorial title from instructions, pricing or legal conditions that should stay stable. Decide whether a line reveal, mask, scale or position change is enough.

## Execute

Set the final readable composition first. Animate decorative representations without duplicating the accessible text. Keep logical reading order and avoid moving essential copy into a canvas-only scene. Define a readable hold, a complete reduced-motion state and cleanup that restores text when animation is interrupted.

Account for font loading, line wrapping, translation and user zoom. Character/word splitting must be rebuilt or avoided when measurement changes. Do not assume a Slovak word and its translation occupy the same number of characters. Keep text motion separate from route focus and form status announcements.

## Verify

Pause mid-reveal, resize, fail the font load and change the motion preference. Check that a user entering the section halfway can still read the full meaning. Verify copy/paste and accessible naming where appropriate. Inspect contrast over changing imagery at each meaningful frame.

## Deliver

Provide text, typography roles, line-wrap strategy, transition phases and semantic/fallback treatment. Designer prompts must ask for complete stable frames and mobile behavior, not only a spectacular title animation. Remove the effect if it delays comprehension or makes the main action harder to find.
