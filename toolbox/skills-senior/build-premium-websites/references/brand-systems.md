# 018 — Značka a jednotný vizuálny systém

Use when the site needs a recognizable identity or several surfaces must feel coherent. Work from the real brand assets and audience rather than a default luxury aesthetic.

## Decide

Define a few observable traits: editorial or utilitarian typography, intimate or expansive crop, quiet or energetic motion, precise or organic geometry. Tie each to the business and actual media. Avoid treating black backgrounds, gold accents and oversized serif text as universal signals of quality.

## Execute

Select typography roles, palette roles, spatial rhythm, image treatment and one repeatable visual motif. Demonstrate them in a hero, an information-dense section and a transactional state. Define boundaries: where the motif is strong, where it becomes quiet and which actions must stay conventional.

Keep semantic tokens separate from raw colors and sizes. Record logo clear space and variants from supplied guidelines; do not invent official brand rules. Preserve honest photography and product appearance. Coordinate the motion language with identity, but keep readable holds and reduced-motion equivalents.

## Verify

Review the same content across desktop, mobile, loading, error and CMS edge cases. Ensure identity survives when a hero video is unavailable. Check whether buttons and links remain identifiable without the accent color. A distinctive site should still be legible when the decorative layer is removed.

## Deliver

Supply a concise visual contract, token mapping, approved examples and source asset references. Specify which decisions are system rules and which are page-specific compositions. Do not force every section into identical cards merely to claim consistency; consistency concerns relationships and behavior, not uniformity of every layout.
