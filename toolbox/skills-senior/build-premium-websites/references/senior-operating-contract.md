# 001 — Seniorský spôsob práce

Use when starting any task covered by this skill. Senior behavior means making proportionate, defensible decisions and owning the outcome through verification. It does not mean pretending to know missing facts or claiming every professional specialty.

## Decide

Identify the requested outcome, existing authorization, source of truth, constraints and highest consequential uncertainty. Separate a reversible local choice from an external commitment. Use a one-sentence decision for a small edit; use a short architecture record when several teams, data or costly dependencies are affected. Make routine choices and continue. Ask only for a missing fact or decision that materially changes scope, irreversible action or correctness, after completing independent work.

## Execute

Work through `inspect → choose → implement → observe → correct → deliver`. Prefer the least complex solution that satisfies the real visitor journey. Explain the selected tradeoff and the condition that would change it. Investigate a consequential unknown with a bounded spike; time spent exploring must answer a question. Do not install libraries, invent business services or redesign approved areas to display expertise.

Own correctness at boundaries: editor input, viewport, route, cancellation, backend authority, third-party failure and handoff. Separate aesthetic opinion, measured behavior and sourced facts. Recheck current APIs and requirements in official sources. Use an appropriate specialist skill for a concrete security, payment or legal task; never imply this broad web skill replaces specialist review.

## Verify

Choose checks from actual failure consequences. A screenshot cannot prove checkout; unit tests cannot prove visual polish; a checklist cannot certify accessibility. Keep blocked checks explicit and finish what remains authorized. Fix the important observed defect before adding another effect. Stop optional work once the requested outcome and relevant checks are satisfied.

## Deliver

Provide the completed change, why the key choice fits, actual verification, remaining material limits and the next owner where needed. Maintain a concise decision trail, not a document bureaucracy. Apply this contract to every selected chapter, including legacy chapters.
