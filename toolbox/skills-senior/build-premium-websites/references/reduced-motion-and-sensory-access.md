# 076 — Obmedzený pohyb a zmyslová prístupnosť

Use whenever animation, autoplay or flashing could affect comfort or comprehension. Reduced motion changes the experience's motion behavior, not its usefulness.

## Decide

Classify effects as essential to a task or decorative. Choose a stable alternative for parallax, large camera travel, rapid scaling and endless loops. Check applicable criterion levels and conditions; do not conflate all motion guidance into a claim of automatic conformance.

## Execute

Read the user's preference and respond if it changes during use. Cancel or finish decorative motion into a clear final state without losing selected data or focus. Offer appropriate pause/stop controls for automatic moving content. Avoid stroboscopic designs instead of trying to justify them through aesthetic intent.

Keep equivalent information and controls in the reduced mode. Replace a camera journey with useful still views or sequential content rather than deleting the explanation. Audio remains opt-in and meaningful content needs the relevant alternatives. Do not make a user hunt through a long animation to find its off switch.

## Verify

Test the preference at initial load and during an active transition, including reverse scroll and route changes. Check that cancellation does not leave opacity zero or invisible click blockers. Inspect pause state, resumed state and static media fallback. Record actual conditions tested.

## Deliver

Provide an effect-by-effect alternative and control policy. State which accessibility checks were performed and which remain open. User comfort is a design constraint from the beginning, not a stylesheet appended after the final demo.
