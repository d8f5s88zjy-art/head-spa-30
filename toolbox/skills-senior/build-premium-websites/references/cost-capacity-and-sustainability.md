# 097 — Náklady, kapacita a efektívnosť

Use when traffic, media or integrations create ongoing costs. Connect estimates to actual usage assumptions.

## Decide

Identify cost drivers: bandwidth, media transformations, database/query load, storage, third-party operations and support. Distinguish average usage from bursts. Use current provider pricing only after verification and label estimates clearly.

## Execute

Model a few plausible scenarios with units, volume and price inputs. Track fixed versus variable costs and the assumptions that dominate uncertainty. Cache safe reusable assets, bound media derivatives and avoid unnecessary per-visitor computation. Prefer the simplest infrastructure that meets required reliability and operations.

For capacity, test representative work and bottlenecks before scaling blindly. Establish safe rate/size limits and graceful degradation. Do not claim environmental savings from fewer bytes without a defensible measurement method; efficient delivery is useful without inventing carbon figures.

## Verify

Compare estimates with actual observed usage where available. Check whether omitted egress, retries, backups or provider minimums materially change the result. Use authorized load tests only within the permitted environment and scale; do not stress live third parties without permission.

## Deliver

Provide assumptions, scenario estimates, current source dates, cost ownership and warning thresholds. Do not turn a small site into a distributed platform solely to appear scalable. A predictable bill and understandable operating model are part of professional developer value.
