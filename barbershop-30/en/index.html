    Before you come

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I book?

    Online any time: pick a service, a barber and a free time. Or call 0951 267 203, on weekdays from 9:00 to 19:00 we always pick up.

    Should I call the barber or the reception?

    Always the reception: 0951 267 203, on weekdays from 9:00 to 19:00 we always pick up. The receptionist looks after your appointment and your comfort, the barber after your result. With a customer in the chair, his attention is on them, not on the phone. It'll be the same when it's you in the chair. The reception finds you an appointment; let them know about a change or cancellation as soon as you can.

    Can I choose my barber?

    Yes, you choose your barber when you book.

    Do you cut kids' hair?

    Yes, the little gentlemen's cut is for boys up to 10: a classic cut or fade, a wash and styling.

    Do you do beards and razor shaves?

    Yes: beard trims with a hot towel and razor, the Hot Towel clean shave and beard colouring.

    Are the prices in the price list final?

    Yes, the prices in the price list are final.

    Do you colour hair too?

    Colouring, highlights and perms are done by Romana. You book them separately on Booqme, where you'll also find their prices.

    What if I can't make it?

    Let us know as soon as you can on 0951 267 203, on weekdays from 9:00 to 19:00 we always pick up.

    What happens when I arrive?

    The receptionist greets you at the door. In the waiting area at the back there's free coffee or mineral water. In the chair it starts with a consultation, then clippers, scissors and a fade; for the beard, a hot towel and a razor. Styling and cologne to finish.

    What if I'm not happy with it?

    You get up from the chair only when you're happy. Whatever doesn't sit right, say so right there in the chair: we agree on it and fix it on the spot. Everything else goes through the reception: 0951 267 203, on weekdays from 9:00 to 19:00 we always pick up.

    When are you open?

    Monday to Friday 9:00–19:00, Saturday 9:00–14:00, closed on Sunday.
